# Launch checklist

> Used in [Phase 6 — Launch](../playbook/06-launch.md). Walked through aloud by the accountable owner with the team. Every unchecked box either blocks launch or gets a written exception below.

**Product:** &nbsp;&nbsp; **Launch date:** &nbsp;&nbsp; **Accountable owner:**

## Evidence

- [ ] Eval results meet pre-agreed thresholds ([eval plan](eval-plan.md) attached)
- [ ] Red-team findings fixed or formally accepted in the [risk register](risk-register.md)
- [ ] Risk register reviewed this week; no open Critical risks

## Observability

- [ ] Dashboards live: errors, latency, cost per interaction, refusal rate
- [ ] Alerts configured and **test-fired** (an alert that has never fired is a hope)
- [ ] In-product user feedback channel working end to end

## Rollout and reversal

- [ ] Rollout stages written: internal → cohort → all, with promotion criteria
- [ ] Rollback rehearsed successfully; takes minutes, not meetings
- [ ] Spend caps / rate limits active

## Users

- [ ] Users told they're interacting with AI, in-product
- [ ] Limitations and "how to reach a human" visible
- [ ] Data-use explanation published and accurate

## Incident readiness

- [ ] Incident plan exists: severity levels, who's paged, comms template
- [ ] Kill-switch owner named; kill-switch tested
- [ ] On-call for launch week agreed

## ⚕ Regulated overlay (skip only if Phase 2 classified as not applicable)

- [ ] Regulatory clearance / registration confirmed in writing
- [ ] Marketing and in-product claims match validated claims **exactly**
- [ ] Adverse-event reporting wired into the incident process

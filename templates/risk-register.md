## How to use

- **Severity** = how bad if it happens: **Critical** (harm to a person's health/money/legal standing, or legal exposure), **Major** (product materially wrong or trust damaged), **Minor** (annoyance, recoverable).
- **Likelihood**: High / Medium / Low — your honest estimate, revised as evidence arrives (evals and red-teaming in Phase 5 are evidence).
- **Status**: **Open** (no mitigation yet) / **Mitigated** (control in place, tested) / **Accepted** (deliberately living with it — named person, written reason).
- Common AI-specific risks to check you've considered: confabulation presented as fact; personal-data leakage in prompts or logs; harmful/biased output for specific user groups; prompt injection; over-reliance by users; provider model change altering behaviour; runaway cost. *(NIST Generative AI Profile)*
